Privacy Policy — Tiny Habits

Last updated: 2025-09-02

1. Overview

Tiny Habits is a personal habit tracker that lets you create habits, schedule daily/weekly reminders, and track completion. This policy explains how Tiny Habits handles your data.

2. What data we collect
  • We do not collect personal data.
  • Your habits, schedules, and progress remain on your device. They are saved locally using on‑device storage.
3. Data storage and processing
  • All habit data is stored locally on your device via the app’s on‑device storage.
  • The app does not transmit your data to our servers or to third parties.
  • If you choose to export your data (e.g., JSON export), the file is created locally and you control where it is saved or shared.
4. Permissions and how we use them
  • Notifications: Used to show local reminders for your habits.
  • Alarms & reminders / exact alarms (Android 12+): Used to schedule time‑accurate reminders.
  • Timezone access: Used to schedule reminders at the correct local time and handle timezone changes.

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or device identifiers.

5. Third‑party services
  • The app does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries.
  • No data is shared with third parties.
6. Children’s privacy

Tiny Habits is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor has provided data, contact us and we will assist with deletion.

7. Your choices and controls
  • You can delete habits or uninstall the app to remove local data.
  • You can export your data locally for your own backup.
  • You can turn notifications on/off in system settings or within the app.
8. Data security

Because data stays on your device, please protect your device with appropriate security (e.g., passcode). We do not operate servers that hold your data.

9. International users

We do not transfer or process your data on remote servers. All processing occurs locally on your device.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will update the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be reflected in the latest version of this page.
